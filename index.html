Royal Gold Vault

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This policy explains how the Royal Gold Vault game handles information.

View Privacy Policy Online

Information and local storage

The game does not ask for personal information or require an account. It saves your scores, unlocked levels, daily challenge history and streaks, achievements and unlock dates, and sound, haptic and animation preferences in the app’s local storage. These records let the game restore your progress and settings.

Servers, analytics and advertising

The game does not send your information to a server operated by the developer or to third parties. It has no developer-operated cloud sync, analytics, advertising or tracking. Daily challenges are generated on the device.

Retention and deletion

The game keeps its local records between sessions and does not automatically delete them. It does not offer an in-app reset. To remove the app and its local records from your device, use Delete App in iOS. Offloading the app keeps its data. Copies in device backups are managed separately through your Apple device or backup settings.

Children’s privacy

The game does not ask children or other players to provide personal information and does not collect it through the game. It does not ask for a date of birth.

Policy updates

If the game’s privacy practices change, this policy will be updated in Settings and in the public copy when available.
